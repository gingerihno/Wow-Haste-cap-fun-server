# Wow-Haste-cap-fun-server
Haste Cap: a solo WotLK 3.3.5a progression server. Climb T1-T10 gear by farming Sigils in 9 custom dungeons, chase the 9837 haste cap and team up to slay 5 world bosses for 2x T10 loot and a 5% Thunderfury. Custom gems and enchants, 20 visual shirts, Legion-style AoE loot, an all-in-one Mall and the HasteCapUI addon.
